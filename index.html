Privacy Policy — Waterbud: Drink Water Reminder

Effective Date: November 15, 2026
Developer: Vishnu Apps
Dedicated URL: https://rmvishnuvardhanreddy.github.io/appspolicy/waterbud/

Waterbud ("we", "our", or "the App") is an offline-first hydration tracking and reminder application for Android and Wear OS. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All of your personal hydration data is stored 100% locally on your device in an on-device database (Android Room & DataStore):

  • Daily water and beverage intake logs
  • Custom cup sizes and names
  • Body weight, activity level, and daily hydration goal
  • Reminder schedule and quiet-hour preferences

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not operate any external servers that collect, store, or transmit your hydration logs.

2. Optional Permissions & Integrations

a) Google Health Connect (android.permission.health.WRITE_HYDRATION)

  • If you explicitly enable "Sync to Health Connect" in Settings, Waterbud writes your logged water intake to Android's on-device Health Connect hub (HydrationRecord).
  • The use of information received from or written to Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Health Connect data is never transmitted off your device by Waterbud or used for advertising or analytics.

b) Approximate Location for Weather Bonus (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

  • If you explicitly enable "Adjust goal on hot days" in Settings, Waterbud reads your device's approximate (coarse) coordinates once per day to query the public Open-Meteo weather API (https://api.open-meteo.com) for current local temperature.
  • Your coordinates are rounded to ~1 km resolution, are used solely to calculate the daily weather hydration bonus, and are never stored on external servers or shared with advertisers.

c) Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

  • Used solely to deliver local hydration reminders according to your configured interval and quiet hours.

d) Backup & Restore

  • You can optionally export or import your hydration history as a JSON or CSV file using the Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework). You control where those files are saved.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

The free version of Waterbud displays ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and manages regional privacy consent via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

  • Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:
    • Device or other IDs (such as the Android Advertising ID)
    • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
    • App interactions and diagnostic data (ad impressions, clicks, and crash/performance telemetry)
  • All data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit via HTTPS.
  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and applicable jurisdictions are presented with a GDPR consent choice before ads are requested.
  • For details on how Google processes data, please see Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • Upgrading to Waterbud Premium permanently disables all ads.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all of your hydration records and settings are stored locally on your device:

  • You can delete individual drink entries at any time inside the app.
  • You can permanently erase all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Waterbud → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Waterbud is intended for general audiences (18+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Medical Disclaimer

Waterbud provides general hydration estimates for healthy adults based on weight, activity level, and ambient temperature. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional if you have kidney disease, heart failure, or medical fluid restrictions.
